Does ICE Ready work without an internet connection?
Yes — fully offline once documents are loaded. You can show proof of status with zero cellular bars or Wi-Fi.
Does ICE Ready add encryption?
ICE Ready doesn't add its own third-party encryption layer — it stores documents inside the operating system's private app sandbox, secured by a dedicated in-app PIN, triple-tap lock, and 30-second auto-lock.
Can law enforcement access the rest of my phone?
No. ICE Ready is designed with strict auto-lock and PIN protections so you can display an ID card without handing over an unlocked phone.
